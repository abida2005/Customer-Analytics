💡 What is this report telling me?

This report answers one simple question: "Out of every new customer I got in a month, how many of them came back to buy again next month, and the month after?" It helps you understand if your shop is building loyal customers or losing them quickly.

📖 How to Read This Report — Simple Guide
📅
First Purchase Month

The month when a group of customers came to your shop for the very first time.

👤
New Customers

How many customers made their very first purchase in that month at your shop.

🔁
After 1, 2, 3 Months

Out of those new customers, how many of the same people came back to buy again.

Colour Guide
